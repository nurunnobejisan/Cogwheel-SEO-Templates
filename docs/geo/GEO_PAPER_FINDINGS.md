# GEO paper findings (KDD 2024)

Source: *GEO: Generative Engine Optimization*, Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024.
Paper: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735> · Benchmark: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/GEO-Optim/geo-bench>

These notes are a literature summary, not a measurement of any client's
visibility. Numbers from the paper are reported as the paper's findings and are
not reused as our own benchmarks.

## What the paper studied

The authors define GEO as optimizing content for generative engines (answer
engines), where the output is a synthesized answer with citations rather than a
ranked list of links. They introduced **GEO-bench**, a 10,000-query benchmark
assembled from nine real-world and synthetic sources and categorised by domain,
difficulty, and intent.

## Method families that improved visibility

- **Cite sources** — give claims a verifiable origin.
- **Add quotations** — attribute statements to named people or documents.
- **Add statistics** — include numbers with units and dates.
- **Improve fluency** — make the answer easy to read and understand.

The paper reports relative visibility improvements up to roughly 40% for some
methods. It also reports that keyword stuffing performed poorly.

## How Cogwheel uses this

| Paper finding | Our implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Cite sources | Citability `citations` component counts external links; provenance gate requires claims to trace to a source |
| Quotations | `citations` component counts quoted passages |
| Statistics | `citations` component counts percentages and unit-bearing numbers |
| Fluency | `answer_coverage` rewards direct answer blocks and question-led headings |
| Structured facts | `structured_data` rewards schema and FAQ markup |

We do **not** claim that a high citability score causes a citation. Citability is
an `estimated` on-page readiness heuristic. Actual citation measurement requires
a connected visibility provider.

## GEO-bench-style prompts in our gold set

`data/brain-gold-prompts.template.json` is a hand-fill template of buyer-style
prompts. It is intentionally blank: gold labels are supplied by a human reviewer,
never generated by a model.
